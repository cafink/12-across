12 Across

How to Play

Roll 12 six-sided letter dice, then use the letters to form words, crossword-style. Words must be at least three letters long, and every row or column of adjacent letters must form a valid English word. No proper nouns.

Drag a tile to move it, or click/tap on the tile and then the space you want to move it to. Valid words are highlighted as you play. You win when all 12 tiles are used in a single connected crossword.

Every puzzle has at least one solution using ordinary English words.
